<!-- Features of the mod, shown in every release (notes and in-app changelog) under "🌟 Mod features", after the new features. -->
* RLX Patches: every Morphe patch for YouTube, with the RLX settings, icons and recommended defaults
* Voice-over translation with Google and Yandex
* Built-in downloader for videos, audio, thumbnails and subtitles (Save to device), with offline playback and SponsorBlock
* Recommended settings from the first start, restorable at any time with "Restore RLX default settings"
* Two variants with every in-app theme: Amoled (Pure Black / White) and Original (BBK Clone, Modern Dark / Soft Light)
* In-app update check, with stable and development channels
